First release. Less phone, on purpose.

A text-first home screen: up to 10 favorites as a plain list, corner shortcuts, and a choice of four clock styles. The full app list is one swipe away with A–Z fast scroll.

"Ask me" turns typed or spoken requests ("turn on wifi and open spotify") into actions — running entirely on your phone, no server, no account. Bring your own MediaPipe model.

Plus mindful extras: screen time, unlock count, and per-app daily limits.
