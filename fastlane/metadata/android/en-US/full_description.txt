<b>A quiet home for your phone.</b>

Foyer replaces your home screen with the things you actually use: a clock, your favourite apps as plain text, and nothing else. No grids, no folders, no feeds, no icons shouting for attention.

<b>AI that never phones home.</b> Ask it to open apps, place calls, or set things in motion — by voice or by typing. The assistant runs on a small language model that lives entirely on your phone. No server, no account, no analytics: airplane mode changes nothing.

<b>Bring your own model.</b> Pick a small model in the app or load one you trust. Your phone, your weights.

<b>Every app, one flick away.</b> The drawer is a plain text list with an a–z fast-scroll rail built for your thumb. Search is one tap away when you need it.

<b>Clocks with character.</b> Digital, bold, dial, or hands — pick the face your day starts with, or turn the clock off entirely.

<b>Tune it, quietly.</b> Three fonts (inter, mono, serif), a handful of accents, and matte backgrounds in dark or light. Quiet by default, yours by choice.

<b>Free and open source.</b> Foyer is GPL-3.0 with no ads, no tracking, and no engagement tricks. The entire app is on GitHub.
